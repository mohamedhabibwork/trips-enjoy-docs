## 6. Business requirements

Priority uses MoSCoW: M must, S should, C could. Phase is the delivery phase in section 3.3.

### 6.1 Accounts and access (AUTH)

<Steps>
<Step>

**BR-AUTH-01 · M · P1** — Each role registers through its own path (traveler, driver, photographer, organization). There is no combined onboarding form.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-AUTH-02 · M · P1** — Every registration form records the applicant's country.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-AUTH-03 · M · P1** — Accounts verify contact details by one-time code before acting: travelers verify at least one of email or phone; drivers, photographers and organization owners verify both (D-20).

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-AUTH-04 · M · P1** — Sign-in strength follows the role: admins use password plus an authenticator; organization staff use password plus an SMS or authenticator code at every sign-in; travelers and suppliers use password or one-time code; moderators use a staff code.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-AUTH-05 · M · P1** — One person can hold several roles (for example traveler and approved photographer) and switch between them without a second account.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-AUTH-06 · M · P1** — Organization staff can switch between the organizations whose access list includes their email, and see no other organization.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-AUTH-07 · M · P1** — Admin can suspend and reinstate any account. Suspension stops access at once and keeps the data.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-AUTH-08 · M · P5** — A user can request account deletion, with a 30-day grace period during which signing in restores the account.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-AUTH-09 · M · P6** — Users register passkeys (WebAuthn) on their account and from account settings. [OPEN: which platforms ship passkey registration first.]

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-AUTH-10 · M · P6** — Users sign in with a registered passkey as an alternative to their role's password or one-time code method.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-AUTH-11 · S · P6** — A user who loses a device recovers by having the stored passkey removed and re-verifying their identity before registering a new one.

</Step>
</Steps>


### 6.2 Applications and approvals (ONB)

<Steps>
<Step>

**BR-ONB-01 · M · P1 (organizations), P4 (suppliers)** — Organizations, platform drivers and platform photographers apply before they can act. Supplier actions stay blocked until approval.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ONB-02 · M · P1** — All applications reach one admin queue with their documents. Admin can approve, request changes or reject; a rejection needs a reason.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ONB-03 · M · P1** — The median decision time is 2 working days or less; overdue applications are flagged.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ONB-04 · M · P1** — When changes are requested, the applicant has 14 days to respond, with a reminder on day 7 and one 7-day extension on request (D-235). Unanswered applications close.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ONB-05 · M · P1** — A rejected applicant can appeal within 14 days of the notice (statement and up to 5 files, decided within 5 working days, one appeal per rejection, D-237) and can re-apply after 30 days. A closed application counts as rejected. The traveler account keeps working.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ONB-06 · M · P4** — Photographer applications include at least 8 portfolio photos.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ONB-07 · M · P4** — Drivers using their own vehicle give plate, model, year, seat count, registration and insurance valid for at least 3 more months.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ONB-08 · M · P4** — Driver applications record consent to lab tests and include an initial lab result dated within the last 30 days.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ONB-09 · M · P1** — Applicants are notified of every status change in their language.

</Step>
</Steps>


The approval journey every application follows:

<Steps>
<Step>

**Apply.** Organizations, platform drivers and platform photographers apply with their documents; supplier actions stay blocked until approval ([BR-ONB-01](/en/docs/brd/requirements#62-applications-and-approvals-onb), [BR-ONB-02](/en/docs/brd/requirements#62-applications-and-approvals-onb)).

</Step>
<Step>

**Review.** Every application lands in one admin queue; the median decision time is 2 working days or less, and overdue applications are flagged ([BR-ONB-02](/en/docs/brd/requirements#62-applications-and-approvals-onb), [BR-ONB-03](/en/docs/brd/requirements#62-applications-and-approvals-onb)).

</Step>
<Step>

**Decision.** Admin approves, requests changes or rejects — a rejection always carries a reason, and applicants are notified in their language at every status change ([BR-ONB-02](/en/docs/brd/requirements#62-applications-and-approvals-onb), [BR-ONB-09](/en/docs/brd/requirements#62-applications-and-approvals-onb)).

</Step>
<Step>

**Changes requested.** The applicant has 14 days to respond, with a reminder on day 7 and one 7-day extension on request. Unanswered applications close ([BR-ONB-04](/en/docs/brd/requirements#62-applications-and-approvals-onb)).

</Step>
<Step>

**Appeal or re-apply.** A rejected applicant can appeal within 14 days (statement, up to 5 files, decided within 5 working days, one appeal per rejection) or re-apply after 30 days. A closed application counts as rejected; the traveler account keeps working throughout ([BR-ONB-05](/en/docs/brd/requirements#62-applications-and-approvals-onb), [BRL-06](/en/docs/brd/requirements#7-business-rules)).

</Step>
</Steps>

### 6.3 Organizations and employees (ORG)

<Steps>
<Step>

**BR-ORG-01 · M · P1** — Each approved organization gets an isolated workspace that no other organization can see.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ORG-02 · M · P1** — The owner invites employees and grants each a permission set (trips, bookings, operations, accounting, coupons, employees, fleet, subscription, integrations, analytics). Employee count follows the plan.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ORG-03 · M · P1** — Organizations can onboard their own drivers and photographers, visible only to them and accepted on assignment without admin approval.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ORG-04 · M · P2** — Organizations register their vehicles (plate, model, year, seats, registration status).

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ORG-05 · M · P3** — Organizations choose whether travelers can pick their own seats, per trip or for the account, where the plan allows.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ORG-06 · S · P6** — Organizations can connect a CRM and send trip, booking and check-in events to external apps through signed webhooks.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ORG-07 · M · P5** — Organizations issue B2B and B2C invoices for their trips; corrections are made by credit note.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ORG-08 · C · P6** — Enterprise organizations can apply custom branding to traveler-facing trip pages, emails and invoices.

</Step>
</Steps>


### 6.4 Trips, programs and fleet (TRIP, PRG, FLT)

<Steps>
<Step>

**BR-TRIP-01 · M · P2** — Organizations create trips through a guided wizard: basics, schedule and route, vehicles, staffing, programs, seating, pricing and capacity, publishing.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-TRIP-02 · M · P2** — A trip can be saved as a hidden draft, published publicly, or published to targeted travelers only.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-TRIP-03 · M · P3** — A trip can run several vehicles at once, each with one vehicle type, one driver and one moderator. The vehicle count follows the plan.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-TRIP-04 · M · P3** — Programs can be added, edited and removed until the trip starts.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-PRG-01 · M · P3** — Each program has one pricing model: free, per person, by quantity, per group, or tiered. Program count follows the plan.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-PRG-02 · M · P3** — A program can be optional, pre-required (auto-included), capacity-limited or VIP-only.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-PRG-03 · M · P3** — Travelers see a live booking total as they add or remove programs, including their plan's program discount.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-PRG-04 · M · P3** — Organizations see who chose each program, quantities and revenue. Report depth by plan is [OD-25](/en/docs/brd/compliance#11-open-decisions).

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-FLT-01 · M · P2** — Vehicle types are reusable seat layouts with labeled seats.

</Step>
</Steps>


### 6.5 Staffing and deals (STF)

<Steps>
<Step>

**BR-STF-01 · M · P3** — Each vehicle is driven either by an organization-owned driver or by a platform driver hired through a deal.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-STF-02 · M · P4** — A trip's photographer is either organization-owned or a platform photographer hired through a deal.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-STF-03 · M · P4** — Organizations and platform suppliers negotiate the price of a deal: either side proposes, counters or rejects, with no round limit. An offer expires 72 hours after the last counter ([BRL-26](/en/docs/brd/requirements#7-business-rules)). Platform suppliers are available only on plans that include them.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-STF-04 · M · P4** — A platform supplier is assigned only after both parties accept the same terms.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-STF-05 · M · P3** — A trip is Ready only when every vehicle has a confirmed driver and moderator and every platform deal is finalized.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-STF-06 · M · P4** — Suppliers see trip, route, date and pay before they respond to a deal.

</Step>
</Steps>


### 6.6 Seats (SEAT)

<Steps>
<Step>

**BR-SEAT-01 · M · P3** — Seating is company-assigned by default. Self-selection is available only on Growth and Enterprise and only where the organization enables it.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-SEAT-02 · M · P3** — With self-selection on, travelers see a live seat map where taken seats show as unavailable immediately.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-SEAT-03 · S · P3** — Explorer and VIP travelers get a priority window for seat selection (length [OPEN: D-316]).

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-SEAT-04 · M · P3** — A seat chosen at checkout is held for 15 minutes and released automatically if payment does not complete.

</Step>
</Steps>


### 6.7 Booking and payment (BKG)

<Steps>
<Step>

**BR-BKG-01 · M · P2** — A booking holds the passengers, chosen programs, seats where allowed, at most one coupon unless the coupon is stackable, and a payment method.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-BKG-02 · M · P2** — Travelers pay on the gateway's own page or SDK, with the methods of the organization's market. Card data never reaches Trips Enjoy.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-BKG-03 · M · P2** — A failed payment can be retried without losing the cart while the hold lasts, up to 3 attempts per hold (D-197). A retry after the hold expires re-checks the seats and starts a new hold only if they are free (D-191).

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-BKG-04 · M · P2** — Travelers can cancel a booking and receive a refund under the cancellation policy ([BRL-12](/en/docs/brd/requirements#7-business-rules)). Requests after the cut-off go to the organization, which decides within 2 working days, then to admin (D-151). No cancellation after departure or after check-in (D-152, D-153).

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-BKG-05 · M · P2** — Every confirmed booking yields a receipt and an invoice the traveler can view. A traveler who enters a tax number at checkout gets a B2B invoice ([BRL-28](/en/docs/brd/requirements#7-business-rules)).

</Step>
</Steps>


### 6.8 Coupons (CPN)

<Steps>
<Step>

**BR-CPN-01 · M · P5** — Admins, organizations, drivers and photographers create coupons that apply only inside their own scope.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-CPN-02 · M · P5** — Coupon use across entity boundaries is always rejected with a generic message that does not name the other entity ([OD-20](/en/docs/brd/compliance#11-open-decisions)).

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-CPN-03 · M · P5** — Coupons support fixed amount, percentage, program-specific, free program, first booking and minimum spend types, and are validated in real time at checkout.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-CPN-04 · M · P5** — Each redemption is recorded against its booking and reversed on refund.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-CPN-05 · M · P5** — Creators see coupon analytics; admin sees platform-wide analytics and abuse flags. Flags never block automatically and are reviewed within 2 working days (D-217).

</Step>
</Steps>


### 6.9 Check-in, moderators and tracking (CHK, MOD, TRK)

<Steps>
<Step>

**BR-CHK-01 · M · P3** — Moderators check each passenger in at boarding and out at each stop and at the destination.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-CHK-02 · M · P3** — A passenger can be checked in only to the vehicle they are assigned to.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-CHK-03 · M · P3** — Every check-in and check-out records time, moderator, vehicle and location where permitted, and reaches the platform within 60 seconds, including after short connection loss.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-CHK-04 · M · P3** — A moderator sees and acts on their assigned vehicle only.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-CHK-05 · S · P3** — Moderators, parents and staff can report incidents and child-safety concerns, which reach the organization's operations team at once. Incident categories: safety, medical, vehicle fault, lost traveler, conduct, other (D-331).

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-MOD-01 · M · P3** — At the end of the trip each moderator submits a report with the final headcount; a mismatch needs a reason.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-TRK-01 · M · P6** — Operations staff see each vehicle's live location and which vehicle each passenger is in. Travelers never see the live vehicle position ([BRL-29](/en/docs/brd/requirements#7-business-rules)).

</Step>
</Steps>


### 6.10 Parents and children (PAR)

<Steps>
<Step>

**BR-PAR-01 · M · P6** — Parents see only their own linked children: vehicle, seat, check-in status and programs. Child data is never used for marketing, advertising or model training.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-PAR-02 · M · P6** — Parents link a child to a booking with a valid booking reference and a guardianship declaration (D-126). The link ends when the booking is cancelled or refunded.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-PAR-03 · M · P6** — Parents are notified when boarding is confirmed, the trip starts, a stop is reached and the trip ends.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-PAR-04 · M · P6** — Child tracking is available where the parent is on Explorer or VIP and the organization is on Growth or Enterprise with tracking enabled for the trip ([OD-05](/en/docs/brd/compliance#11-open-decisions)). Live position is shown only while the trip is In Progress (D-317).

</Step>
</Steps>


### 6.11 Media and photographers (MED, PHO)

<Steps>
<Step>

**BR-MED-01 · M · P4** — Photographers upload albums after the trip is Completed, within 14 days (D-267). Album count per trip follows the plan.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-MED-02 · M · P4** — Albums use one of three privacy modes allowed by the photographer's plan: public to trip participants, shared link, or selected users.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-MED-03 · S · P4** — Studio and Pro photographers can watermark public copies (bottom right, inset 3 percent, 30 percent opacity, D-275).

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-MED-04 · M · P4** — Signed-in travelers who took the trip view the albums they are allowed to see. Public albums allow download of the watermarked or medium copy; shared-link and selected-user albums do not offer download (D-264, D-266).

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-MED-05 · M · P4** — Media is the platform's single file store for every domain's uploads (application documents, portfolio photos, album photos, and any other file). A file that is not linked to its owning entity and not created by the system is deleted automatically 24 hours after upload ([BRL-31](/en/docs/brd/requirements#7-business-rules)).

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-PHO-01 · M · P4** — Photographers manage portfolio, rates and availability (dates and ranges, no weekly rules, D-89) and see earnings and payouts.

</Step>
</Steps>


### 6.12 Notifications (NTF)

<Steps>
<Step>

**BR-NTF-01 · M · P1** — Notifications go out on the channels each user enables per category: email, SMS, WhatsApp, push and in-app.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-NTF-02 · M · P1** — Notifications use the user's language, with right-to-left layout for Arabic.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-NTF-03 · S · P3** — SMS and push respect quiet hours 22:00 to 07:00 local time, except urgent messages; deferred messages go at 07:00, and trip-day events older than 2 hours are dropped (D-290).

</Step>
</Steps>


### 6.13 Plans and subscriptions (SUB)

<Steps>
<Step>

**BR-SUB-01 · M · P5** — Admin defines plans per role and market: limits, features, monthly and annual prices, annual discount and trial length.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-SUB-02 · M · P5** — Plan limits and features are enforced automatically. A blocked action shows an upgrade prompt. Every non-traveler role needs an active plan to use the app ([BRL-02](/en/docs/brd/requirements#7-business-rules)).

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-SUB-03 · M · P5** — Failed renewals trigger dunning and a 7-day grace period before the account loses plan features.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-SUB-04 · M · P5** — Admin sees recurring revenue, churn, upgrades, downgrades and plan distribution.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-SUB-05 · M · P5** — Admin can grant, revoke or change a plan for one account, with a reason, a second admin's approval and an end date.

</Step>
</Steps>


### 6.14 Accounting, invoices and settlements (ACC)

<Steps>
<Step>

**BR-ACC-01 · M · P5** — Organizations see, per trip, base revenue, program revenue, coupon impact, supplier costs, commission and gateway fees. Depth follows the plan.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ACC-02 · M · P5** — Admin sees system-wide finance: subscription revenue, commission, deal audit, coupon redemptions, disputes and payouts.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ACC-03 · M · P4** — Suppliers see their earnings and receive weekly payouts. A negative balance is netted against the next payout in the same currency (D-173).

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ACC-04 · M · P5** — Exports in CSV and PDF follow the plan; admin system-wide exports are CSV only; export files expire after 7 days (D-249).

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ACC-05 · M · P2** — Every financial event is recorded in a double-entry ledger and reconciled daily with gateway records (D-29).

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ACC-06 · M · P4** — The platform records amounts owed between the platform, organizations and suppliers per trip and tracks each settlement to completion. A settlement opens when the trip is Completed and closes when every payout is made and no dispute is open (D-174).

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ACC-07 · M · P5** — Settlements and invoices cover every party pair: traveler to organization (B2C or B2B), organization to supplier (deal), platform to organization and supplier (commission and payout), and organization or supplier to platform (plan).

</Step>
</Steps>


### 6.15 Platform administration (ADM)

<Steps>
<Step>

**BR-ADM-01 · M · P1** — Admin reviews all applications and appeals from one queue.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ADM-02 · M · P1** — Admin manages any account: suspend, reinstate, reset credentials, review deletion and data-copy requests.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ADM-03 · M · P4** — Admin can require any approved driver to take a lab test at any time; the driver is paused until the result is accepted ([BRL-16](/en/docs/brd/requirements#7-business-rules), [BRL-17](/en/docs/brd/requirements#7-business-rules)). Lab tests apply to drivers only (D-98).

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ADM-04 · M · P3** — Admin records disputes, decides outcomes and issues refunds, including refunds outside policy with a reason.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ADM-05 · M · P1** — Every admin action and permission change is written to an audit log no one can edit.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ADM-06 · M · P1** — Admin manages plans, coupons, notification templates, feature flags and market settings.

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ADM-07 · M · P3** — Disputes are acknowledged within 2 working days and resolved within 10 working days; information requests are answered within 5 working days. Types: booking, payment, refund, supplier conduct, organization conduct, other (D-32, D-254).

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ADM-08 · M · P3** — Admin, finance and sensitive actions are written to an immutable, append-only audit trail no one can edit ([BRL-19](/en/docs/brd/requirements#7-business-rules)).

</Step>
<Step>

**BR-ADM-09 · M · P3** — Audit records are kept 7 years and exportable by admins.

</Step>
</Steps>

**BR-MKT-02 · M · P2** — An account's market is set from the country it registered with. An organization's market decides the currency, payment methods and tax rules of its trips.

</Steps>

Depth follows the plan (Growth basic, Enterprise full).

**BR-ANL-02 · S · P6** — Drivers and photographers see analytics for their own bookings and earnings at the depth of their plan.

</Step>

**BR-API-01 · C · P6** — Organizations and partners can use a documented public API for trips and bookings.

## 7. Business rules

<Steps>
<Step>

**BRL-01** — Trip records are tenant-isolated. Only the owning organization's staff, the platform admin, and the people on a booking within their own scope (traveler, linked parent, assigned moderator, driver or photographer) may see them.

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-02** — Every non-traveler role must hold an active plan to use the app. A traveler can always use the app for free.

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-03** — A coupon applies only inside its creator's scope: admin to any trip; organization to its own trips; driver or photographer to bookings where they are assigned. Cross-entity use is rejected.

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-04** — A platform driver or photographer is assigned only after a finalized deal accepted by both parties.

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-05** — An organization-owned driver or photographer is accepted on assignment and is never visible to other organizations.

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-06** — A rejected supplier or organization application can be re-submitted after 30 days. A closed application counts as rejected. The traveler account keeps working throughout.

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-07** — A trip cannot move to Ready until every vehicle has a driver and a moderator and every platform deal is finalized.

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-08** — A seat hold lasts 15 minutes and is released automatically when it expires. Exception: a Fawry cash voucher holds seats for the voucher's 48-hour window (D-31, D-41).

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-09** — A moderator can check a passenger in only to the vehicle assigned to that passenger on that trip.

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-10** — Parents see data only for children linked to their own bookings.

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-11** — A refund reverses the coupon redemption and frees the traveler's per-user limit.

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-12** — Cancellation refund depends on time before departure: 80 percent at 48 hours or more, 50 percent from 24 to under 48 hours, none under 24 hours (placeholder, D-07, [OD-07](/en/docs/brd/compliance#11-open-decisions)). All fees are refundable until non-refundable fees are set (D-156).

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-13** — A plan upgrade takes effect at once with proration; a downgrade takes effect at the end of the cycle.

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-14** — A failed renewal starts a 7-day grace period with features kept.

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-15** — Account deletion becomes permanent after a 30-day grace period. Trip records are kept 5 years after deletion. Future bookings are cancelled and refunded; remaining earnings are paid on the next payout date. No booking or payment is allowed during the grace period.

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-16** — A lab test has a deadline of 3 days from request (admin may set 1 to 7 days, D-21). A missed deadline suspends the driver; a suspension longer than 30 days closes the driver account. A second failed result within 12 months closes the account (D-242).

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-17** — A driver whose lab test is requested is paused at once. Upcoming trips return to their organizers. Completed trips are paid; trips not yet completed are held (D-175).

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-18** — Every financial event (booking, refund, coupon, subscription charge, payout, deal cost, commission, invoice) is written to a double-entry ledger.

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-19** — Every admin action and every permission change is written to an immutable audit log.

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-20** — Verification codes expire after 5 minutes. After 5 wrong codes, verification pauses for 15 minutes. A resend does not reset the wrong-code count; after 3 failed deliveries the code goes by the other channel (D-58, D-59).

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-21** — Accounts lock for 15 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts (all roles, D-53).

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-22** — Web sessions end after 12 hours of use. Drafts are kept.

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-23** — Every registration form records the applicant's country.

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-24** — A trip's programs, seat mode or vehicles cannot be changed in a way that breaks a confirmed booking until the traveler is notified and offered a remedy. The traveler has 7 days to choose; then the case goes to admin (D-148).

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-25** — A trip is archived 30 days after Completed, and only once its settlement is closed (D-26).

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-26** — A deal offer expires 72 hours after the last counter-offer and the deal ends as Rejected with reason "expired" (D-14).

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-27** — Market values (currency, payment methods, tax and invoicing rules, default time zone) follow the country: the organization's country for its trips and invoices, and the account's country for its own plan.

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-28** — An invoice is B2B when the buyer gives a tax number, otherwise B2C (D-180). An issued invoice is never edited; a correction is a credit note that references it (D-181).

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-29** — Travelers never see a vehicle's live position. Only operations staff of the owning organization, the platform admin and eligible linked parents do, and parents only while the trip is In Progress (D-317).

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-30** — When an organization cancels a trip, every confirmed booking is refunded in full and coupon redemptions are reversed (D-150). A trip that is In Progress cannot be cancelled; it is handled as an incident (D-25, [OD-22](/en/docs/brd/compliance#11-open-decisions)).

</Step>
<Step>

**BRL-31** — Media is the file store for the whole platform. A stored file is kept only while it is linked to its owning entity or created by the system; a file that is neither is deleted automatically, with its derived variants, 24 hours after upload.

</Step>
