### 6.1 Core entities

[ERD](/en/docs/erd) holds the tables, columns, keys, lookup types and RLS paths. This table states each entity's purpose, owner scope and retention. Scope means who owns the rows: tenant (one organization), platform, account (one person) or supplier.

| Entity | Purpose | Scope | ERD tables | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Account and roles | Login identity and held roles | Account | users, user_roles, auth_factors, sessions, devices | Until deletion, then section 6.4 |
| Market | Country configuration | Platform | markets, market_payment_methods, tax_rates | Life of platform |
| Organization | Tenant root and settings | Tenant | organizations | Life of tenant, then financial retention |
| Member and permissions | Staff access per organization | Tenant | organization_members, member_permissions, invitations | Kept with audit log |
| Application | Organization and supplier applications | Platform | applications, application_documents, application_decisions, appeals | [OPEN: rejected applications and documents](/en/docs/recommendations/open-decisions#open-12) |
| Supplier profiles | Driver and photographer details | Platform, or tenant for organization-owned | driver_profiles, photographer_profiles, provider_vehicles, portfolio_items, availability_slots | Life of account |
| Lab test | Driver lab-test record | Platform | lab_tests, lab_partners | Per market legal review |
| Vehicle type and vehicle | Seat layouts and fleet | Tenant | vehicle_types, vehicle_type_seats, vehicles | Life of tenant |
| Trip | One organized trip with stops, audience, vehicles and staff | Tenant | trips, trip_stops, trip_audiences, trip_vehicles, trip_staff_assignments | 5 years after account deletion |
| Program | Activities and tiers | Tenant | programs, program_tiers | As trip |
| Booking | Reservation, passengers, programs, seats, remedies | Tenant and traveler | bookings, booking_passengers, booking_programs, trip_seats, booking_remedies | 7 years (financial) |
| Deal | Negotiation and offers | Tenant and supplier | deals, deal_offers | 7 years (financial) |
| Payment and refund | Gateway payments, attempts, refunds | Tenant or account | payments, payment_attempts, refunds | 7 years |
| Coupon | Codes, scope, redemptions, flags | Creator scope | coupons, coupon_programs, coupon_audiences, coupon_redemptions, abuse_flags | Redemptions 7 years |
| Plan and subscription | Catalog, prices, subscriptions, charges, overrides, counters | Platform and owner | plans, plan_limits, plan_prices, subscriptions, subscription_charges, plan_overrides, usage_counters | Charges 7 years |
| Check-in and tracking | Boarding events, positions, incidents, reports | Tenant | checkin_events, tracking_points, incidents, trip_reports | Check-in 1 year; GPS 90 days |
| Child link and safety case | Parent links and safety cases | Parent and tenant | child_links, safety_cases | Child trip history 1 year |
| Album and photo | Trip media and access | Photographer and trip | albums, photos, album_shared_links, album_members, download_logs | Albums until deleted; download logs 1 year |

| Reconciliation | Gateway differences | Platform | reconciliation_exceptions | 7 years |

| Consent and privacy | Consents and data requests | Account | consents, privacy_requests | Life of account plus 7 years (proposed) |
| Audit | Admin actions and permission changes | Platform | audit_logs | 7 years |

### 6.2 Data isolation

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| [DAT-01](/en/docs/srs/data#62-data-isolation) | Every tenant row carries `organization_id`. Reads and writes are limited to the tenant at the database (RLS), repository and API layers. | Must |
| [DAT-02](/en/docs/srs/data#62-data-isolation) | No user-facing query joins across tenants. Platform-wide reads run under the admin or platform repository and are logged. | Must |
| [DAT-03](/en/docs/srs/data#62-data-isolation) | When deletion becomes permanent, personal fields are removed or replaced with "Deleted user", except records kept under section 6.4. Trip records stay 5 years. Future bookings are cancelled and refunded; remaining earnings are paid on the next payout date. | Must |
| [DAT-04](/en/docs/srs/data#62-data-isolation) | Identity documents and lab results are in a restricted bucket visible only to admins; every open is audited. | Must |
| [DAT-05](/en/docs/srs/data#62-data-isolation) | Phone numbers, ID and license numbers, and payout details are encrypted at column level. | Must |
| [DAT-06](/en/docs/srs/data#62-data-isolation) | Money is stored as minor units plus currency code; rounding follows [FR-MKT-05](/en/docs/srs/requirements/mkt#fr-mkt-05--rounding-and-display). | Must |
| [DAT-10](/en/docs/srs/data#62-data-isolation) | Every API and file path uses the public UUID; internal numeric IDs never leave the backend. | Must |

### 6.3 Soft delete, immutability and deletion

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| [DAT-07](/en/docs/srs/data#63-soft-delete-immutability-and-deletion) | Suspended accounts, cancelled bookings and withdrawn applications are soft-deleted or kept, hidden from user views and visible to admins. | Must |
| [DAT-08](/en/docs/srs/data#63-soft-delete-immutability-and-deletion) | Ledger, audit, deal offer, check-in, tracking, consent and outbox rows are insert-only. Corrections are reversing rows. | Must |
| [DAT-09](/en/docs/srs/data#63-soft-delete-immutability-and-deletion) | A deleted account keeps its trip and financial records for the periods in 6.4, with personal fields removed where the law allows. | Must |
| [DAT-11](/en/docs/srs/data#63-soft-delete-immutability-and-deletion) | Soft delete is uniform: a soft-deletable row carries `deleted_at TIMESTAMPTZ NULL` plus `deleted_by BIGINT NULL` FK `users.id`; there is no boolean flag. A row is deleted when `deleted_at` is not null, and restore clears both columns. | Must |
| [DAT-12](/en/docs/srs/data#63-soft-delete-immutability-and-deletion) | Soft-deleted rows are restorable for 30 days, aligned with the account deletion grace ([BRL-15](/en/docs/brd/requirements#7-business-rules)). After the window the row stays hidden and is removed by the retention purge of section 6.4. | Must |
| [DAT-13](/en/docs/srs/data#63-soft-delete-immutability-and-deletion) | Ledger, audit and every other immutable table of [DAT-08](/en/docs/srs/data#63-soft-delete-immutability-and-deletion) are never soft-deleted (no `deleted_at` column) and are never hard-deleted within their retention periods. | Must |
| [DAT-14](/en/docs/srs/data#63-soft-delete-immutability-and-deletion) | Every soft-deletable table keeps a partial index `where deleted_at is null` for each hot query (list views, active-row lookups, uniqueness) so deleted rows are never scanned. | Must |
| [DAT-15](/en/docs/srs/data#63-soft-delete-immutability-and-deletion) | Unique constraints on soft-deletable tables are partial unique indexes over live rows: `unique (cols) where deleted_at is null`. Where a deleted row's value must stay distinct for history, a companion `unique (cols, deleted_at) where deleted_at is not null` index applies the `deleted_at` tiebreak. | Must |

### 6.4 Retention

| Data | Retention | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Account during deletion grace | 30 days; sign-in restores | [BRL-15](/en/docs/brd/requirements#7-business-rules) |
| Account personal data after deletion | Removed, except records below | [BRL-15](/en/docs/brd/requirements#7-business-rules) |
| Trip records | 5 years after account deletion | [BRL-15](/en/docs/brd/requirements#7-business-rules) |
| Bookings, payments, invoices, ledger, payouts, deals | 7 years | [OD-04](/en/docs/brd/compliance#11-open-decisions) (pending legal review) |
| Audit log | 7 years, object-locked | D-04, BRD 8.5 |
| Check-in and check-out events | 1 year | D-45 |
| Raw GPS points | 90 days, then an hourly summary is kept | Architecture 18.4 |
| Child trip history | 1 year | D-135 |
| Album download logs | 1 year | D-271 |
| Verification codes | 5 minutes | [BRL-20](/en/docs/brd/requirements#7-business-rules) |
| Idempotency keys | 24 hours | [NFR-SEC-06](/en/docs/srs/quality#82-security-nfr-sec) |
| Identity documents and lab results | [OPEN: per market legal review](/en/docs/recommendations/open-decisions#open-13) | BRD 8.2 |
| Notification delivery logs | [OPEN](/en/docs/recommendations/open-decisions#open-13) | Not set |
| Application logs | 7 years in archive storage | Architecture 22.1 |
| Database backups | Daily snapshots 35 days; point-in-time 7 days; cross-region copies 30 days | Architecture 22.1 |
