## FR-TRK-01 — Live vehicle location
Operations staff with trip access shall see each vehicle's live position, updated within 500 ms of arrival.
<ReqMeta priority="Must" source="BR-TRK-01" />
- Given a new GPS point for vehicle 2, when the map is open, then vehicle 2 moves within 500 ms.

## FR-TRK-02 — Location collection
The driver app (or the moderator app as fallback) shall send a point every 5 to 10 seconds only while the trip is In Progress, batching up to 10 points per request and buffering up to 60 minutes offline (D-34, D-121).
<ReqMeta priority="Must" source="BR-TRK-01; ASM-03" />
- Given the trip has not started, when the device is online, then no points are sent.

## FR-TRK-03 — Passenger vehicle view
Operations shall see which vehicle each checked-in passenger is in.
<ReqMeta priority="Must" source="BR-TRK-01" />
- Given P checked in to vehicle 2 and not out, when operations looks up P, then vehicle 2 shows.

## FR-TRK-04 — Per-trip child tracking switch
Parent live tracking shall be available only where the trip's child tracking switch is on ([FR-PAR-03](/en/docs/srs/requirements/par#fr-par-03--eligibility)).
<ReqMeta priority="Must" source="BR-PAR-04" />
- Given the switch is off, when a parent opens the tracker, then no live position shows.

## FR-TRK-05 — Tracking data scope
Positions and passenger movement shall be visible only to the owning organization's staff with operations access, admins, and eligible linked parents.
<ReqMeta priority="Must" source="BRL-01; BRL-29" />
- Given another organization's user requests positions, when processed, then the response is not found.

## FR-TRK-06 — End of tracking
When the trip is marked ended, collection shall stop and an end event go to linked parents and operations.
<ReqMeta priority="Must" source="BR-TRK-01; BR-PAR-03" />
- Given the trip is ended, when a new point arrives, then it is rejected.

## FR-TRK-07 — No live position for travelers
Travelers shall never see a vehicle's live position or ETA; their trip-day view shows the vehicle, seat, check-in status and the schedule (D-317).
<ReqMeta priority="Must" source="BRL-29" />
- Given a traveler on an In Progress trip, when they open trip day, then no map position or distance is shown.
