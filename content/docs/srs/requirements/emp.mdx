## FR-EMP-01 — Permission sets
The owner shall invite employees and grant each a set of permissions in the areas trips, bookings, operations, accounting, coupons, employees, fleet, subscription, integrations and analytics. Templates (Operations, Finance, Support, Manager) are offered as starting points.
<ReqMeta priority="Must" source="BR-ORG-02" />
- Given an employee without accounting permission, when they open accounting, then the area is hidden and direct requests are refused.

## FR-EMP-02 — Permission changes are audited
Every grant, change and removal shall be written to the audit log with actor, time, and before and after values, and shown in the employee's permission history.
<ReqMeta priority="Must" source="BRL-19" />
- Given an owner removes a permission, when saved, then the audit log records owner, employee, time and the removed permission.

## FR-EMP-03 — Employee limit per plan
Active employees shall not exceed the plan limit (Starter 5, Growth 20, Enterprise unlimited).
<ReqMeta priority="Must" source="BR-ORG-02; BR-SUB-02" />
- Given a Starter organization with 5 active employees, when a sixth is invited, then it is blocked with an upgrade prompt.

## FR-EMP-04 — Server-side scope
Every permission shall be enforced by the server on every request.
<ReqMeta priority="Must" source="BR-ORG-02; BRL-01" />
- Given an employee with trip scope only calls an accounting endpoint, when processed, then it is refused as forbidden.

## FR-EMP-05 — Employee removal
Removing an employee shall end their access to that organization at once; past actions and audit entries remain.
<ReqMeta priority="Must" source="BR-ORG-02; BR-AUTH-07" />
- Given a removed employee, when they sign in, then the organization is not listed and audit entries still show their name.

## FR-EMP-06 — Invitation and first sign-in
An invitation link shall go to the employee's email, valid 7 days (D-286). On first sign-in the employee sets a password and enrolls a second factor (SMS or authenticator) before the workspace opens.
<ReqMeta priority="Must" source="BR-ORG-02; BR-AUTH-04" />
- Given an accepted invitation, when the employee signs in for the first time, then second-factor setup is required before the workspace opens.
