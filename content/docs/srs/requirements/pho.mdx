Traces to the [business requirements](/en/docs/brd/requirements).

## FR-PHO-01 — Portfolio
Photographers shall manage the portfolio within the plan allowance (Basic 10, Studio 50, Pro unlimited).
<ReqMeta priority="Must" source="BR-PHO-01; BR-ONB-06" />
- Given 10 images on Basic, when another is uploaded, then it is blocked with an upgrade prompt.

## FR-PHO-02 — Rates and availability
Photographers shall set rates and mark available or unavailable dates and ranges (no weekly rules, D-89). Deals for unavailable dates are refused.
<ReqMeta priority="Must" source="BR-PHO-01" />
- Given a date marked unavailable, when an organization proposes a deal for it, then the proposal is refused.

## FR-PHO-03 — Earnings
Photographers shall see earnings, statements and payouts; commission shows as a ledger line without a printed rate until [OD-15](/en/docs/brd/compliance#11-open-decisions) is decided.
<ReqMeta priority="Must" source="BR-PHO-01; BR-ACC-03" />
- Given a payout was made, when earnings open, then it shows with amount and date.

## FR-PHO-04 — Active bookings by plan
Bookings per month shall follow the plan (Basic 3, Studio 10, Pro unlimited).
<ReqMeta priority="Must" source="BR-SUB-02" />
- Given 3 bookings this month on Basic, when a fourth deal is finalized, then it is blocked with an upgrade prompt.

## FR-PHO-05 — Photographer coupons
Photographers shall create coupons that apply only to bookings where they are assigned.
<ReqMeta priority="Must" source="BR-CPN-01; BRL-03" />
- Given another photographer's booking, when the coupon is applied, then it is refused.

## FR-PHO-06 — Visibility and badge
Profile visibility and the verified badge shall follow the plan; Basic shows no badge.
<ReqMeta priority="Should" source="BR-SUB-02" />
- Given a Basic photographer, when the profile is viewed, then no badge shows.
