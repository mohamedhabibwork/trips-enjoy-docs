This page records the **Locked Resolutions & Configurable System Contracts** for all 32 BRD decisions (`OD-01`..`OD-32`) in [BRD §11](/en/docs/brd/compliance#11-open-decisions) and all **15 inline `[OPEN]` markers** (`OPEN-01`..`OPEN-15`) across the specification repository.

<Callout type="info" title="Sign-Off & Runtime Configurability Status (45 Decided · 2 Deferred)">
Following stakeholder review and formal sign-off in [BRD §12](/en/docs/brd/compliance#12-approval) ([OPEN-01](#open-01)), **all 32 BRD decisions (`OD-01`..`OD-32`) and 13 of 15 inline markers (`OPEN-01`..`OPEN-08`, `OPEN-10`, `OPEN-12`..`OPEN-15`) are now `Decided`**, while [`OPEN-09`](#open-09) (cloud region & data residency) and [`OPEN-11`](#open-11) (CI test coverage floors) are marked `Deferred` until production deployment. By architectural mandate, nearly all commercial, tax, payment, quota, safety, rate-limit, and retention parameters below are **seeded as defaults and dynamically configurable at runtime via Admin Console (`UF-ADM-13`), per-country market settings (`markets`), and per-plan entitlements (`plan_limits`, `plan_prices`)** without code redeployment.
</Callout>
