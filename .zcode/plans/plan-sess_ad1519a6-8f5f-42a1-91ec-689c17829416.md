## Plan (revised per user approval): End-to-end accounting coverage across the platform docs

### Goal
Produce a single executive-summary **accounting view** of the platform — both a top-level workflow doc and per-service accounting sections — so the story of money (transactions, taxes, expenses, government costs) is consistent and complete across every document an accountant, finance reviewer, regulator, or engineer would read.

### What "cleared" means here
Every reader — at any level — can answer four questions without leaving the doc they're reading:
1. **What money facts does this service own or touch?** (transaction, tax, expense, or government cost).
2. **What ledger postings (or other accounting artifacts) does it create or depend on?**
3. **Where does the accounting reconciliation / drift detection happen for it?**
4. **Where does the human operator intervene (admin / support / journal entry)?**

The two layers of output:

---

### Layer 1 — New top-level doc

**File:** `docs/workflows/ACCOUNTING_WORKFLOWS.md`

Same structure as `PAYMENT_WORKFLOWS.md`, but the *accounting* lens (not the operational payment lens). Sections:

1. H1 + intro paragraph: "This document is the single accounting view of the platform — it consolidates the per-service docs and shows how transactions, taxes, expenses, and government costs flow from the customer, through the operational services, into the double-entry ledger, and out to remittance and reporting."
2. **Scope & Boundaries** — what an accounting reader cares about (recognition, accrual, tax, expense, remittance, period close) vs. what is intentionally deferred (operational capture/refund mechanics → `PAYMENT_WORKFLOWS.md` / `REFUND_WORKFLOWS.md`).
3. **Accounting Model** — four-layer truth model (Pricing → Operational → Audit/Ledger → Reporting), with a Mermaid `flowchart` showing the layers.
4. **Actors and Services** table — same style as `PAYMENT_WORKFLOWS.md`.
5. **Workflow sections** (one `## Workflow: <Name>` each, with Mermaid `sequenceDiagram` + short note):
   - **Customer Transaction Recognition (Ride / Food)** — pricing → tax → quote → capture → ledger postings on `cash` / `revenue` / `tax_payable`.
   - **Tax Calculation & Remittance** — `tax.calculated.v1` → integrated into quote → collected → `tax_payable` ledger account → periodic remittance (per-jurisdiction filing) → close to `tax_remitted`.
   - **Driver / Courier Income (Gross-to-Net)** — fare accrual → commission → tax withholding → tip → incentive → net withdrawal → postings on `driver_payable` / `tax_withheld_payable` / `commission_revenue` / `cash`.
   - **Restaurant Settlement & Marketplace VAT** — order completed → merchant payable accrual → commission + VAT recognition → payout scheduled → bank transfer → postings on `merchant_payable` / `commission_revenue` / `tax_payable` / `cash`.
   - **Corporate Income Tax & Regulatory Fees** — periodic CIT calc in `reporting-service` from ledger trial balance → `tax-provision` journal entry → remittance.
   - **Expense Recognition — Incentives, Refunds, Opex, Chargebacks** — single diagram with `alt` branches showing the four expense flows and the resulting `expense_*` ledger accounts (`6100_payment_processing_fees`, `6200_refunds`, `6300_incentive_payments`, `6400_chargeback_losses`).
   - **Reconciliation & Period Close** — daily per-service jobs (payment 02:00, wallet 03:00, courier-earnings 03:00, ledger 04:00 UTC) → drift detection → `ledger.audit.reconciliation_drift.v1` → P1 ticket → month-end close → trial balance → balance sheet / income statement.
6. **Idempotency in Accounting** — table of idempotency keys per accounting action.
7. **Failure Paths Summary** — mandatory table.
8. **Acceptance Criteria** — mandatory bullets with SLOs.
9. **See also** — links to `PAYMENT_WORKFLOWS.md`, `REFUND_WORKFLOWS.md`, `architecture/DATA_OWNERSHIP.md`, `architecture/CONSISTENCY_STRATEGY.md`, `architecture/adrs/0013-double-entry-ledger.md`, `architecture/EVENT_ARCHITECTURE.md`, `architecture/FAILURE_HANDLING.md`, `services/ledger-service/WORKFLOWS.md`, and the affected per-service READMEs.

Conventions enforced (matches `PAYMENT_WORKFLOWS.md` exactly): H1 + intro, `## Actors and Services` table, `## Workflow: <Name>` per scenario, `sequenceDiagram` with `participant <ALIAS> as <service>`, `alt/else/end` branches, `Note over <alias>:` for cron notes, money always `{amount_minor, currency}`, events `.v1` suffixed in backticks, service names in backticks, idempotency keys inline.
